Our mission

Ardenne develops the whole person: mentally, physically, socially, academically and spiritually. Our motto, Deo Duce Quaere Optima, means “With God as Guide, Seek the Best.”

Co-curricular activities

Every student takes part in at least one.

  • Sports: swimming, football, basketball, netball, lawn tennis, badminton, track and field
  • Clubs: ISCF, drama, dance, chess, Key Club, debating, choir
  • Service: cadets, guides, Interact, Ardenne Coalition of Environmentalists
